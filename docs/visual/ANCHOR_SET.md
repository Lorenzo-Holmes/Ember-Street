## A01 — 林夏 / Lin Xia — Core Character Master

### Canonical identity

- Chinese woman, late 20s
- approx. 166 cm, lean but not athletic-model lean
- narrow oval face with slightly prominent cheekbones
- dark brown eyes, modest almond shape, tired lower lids
- straight medium-width nose, not small or stylized
- narrow natural lips, frequently pressed together while thinking
- black hair, roughly jaw-to-neck length, uneven self-trimmed bob, slightly left of center part
- no makeup
- faint healed 18–22 mm scar cutting through the outer end of the **left eyebrow**
- dry skin, light under-eye shadows, dust at hairline and jaw

### Canonical clothing architecture

Must remain recognizable across all future variants:

- faded cold slate-blue/gray hooded work windbreaker, matte cheap fabric
- right elbow repaired with a rectangular dirty-beige cloth patch and visible coarse stitching
- charcoal-gray thin knit layer underneath
- dark worn work trousers with a practical side pocket; not tactical cargo pants
- scuffed low-cut brown-gray walking shoes, mismatched replacement laces acceptable
- small dirty-beige canvas cross-body map satchel worn high at the left hip
- short strip of faded dark-red cloth tied to the satchel strap as her personal marker

### Habitual objects

- folded paper route map
- short carpenter pencil / graphite pencil
- compact weak flashlight clipped to satchel, not held like a weapon

### Body language

She does not stand square to camera. Her shoulders angle slightly toward the nearest exit. Weight is subtly forward, as if she is always checking a route before committing.

Expression: alert, observant, tired, quietly determined. Not grimacing. Not cool. Not fearless.

### Standard composition

- 2:3 vertical
- 3/4 view
- upper thigh crop
- both hands visible: one holding a folded map, one touching/adjusting the satchel strap
- figure occupies 68–72% frame height
- leave clear negative space above shoulders and beside the torso for UI crop flexibility
- simple background: damaged plaster wall, edge of old window, a few non-readable route lines / marks on paper pinned to wall

### Lighting

Cold overcast window light from camera-left, weak tungsten bulb from rear-right. The warm light is only a small accent.

## A03 — 便利店 / Convenience Store — Core Location Master

### Gameplay identity

Primary resource: ration. Secondary: materials. Low early-game danger.

### Mandatory visual structure

Main subject:
- narrow street-front convenience store with a metal rolling shutter stuck half-open

Narrative details (keep 2–4 legible, not cluttered):
- fallen light metal shelf with a few sealed tins / packets still visible
- dusty plastic crates and collapsed cardboard near the front
- dark rear storage doorway with no clear view inside
- old refrigerated case, dead and fogged/dirty, with a few intact sealed packages

Danger cue:
- the half-open shutter forces a crawl / crouch entry and the rear room is unreadable

Do not turn it into a supermarket. It is a small neighborhood shop.

### Composition

- 3:2 or 16:9 landscape
- documentary street-level angle
- storefront/shutter as immediate first read
- reserve roughly left 25% or upper-left as lower-detail UI-safe region
- resource clues readable at card size

### Rendering prompt

The shop is cramped and ordinary, not a large supermarket. Cracked plaster, rusted shutter edges, broken fluorescent fixture, dust, soot and abrasion. Wartime civilian survival field-journal illustration, charcoal sketch, graphite drawing, rough ink, imperfect contour lines, cross-hatching, rubbed charcoal shadows, weathered paper fibers, low saturation charcoal black, concrete gray, dirty beige, brown, cold blue gray, extremely sparse faded red warning smear and dim yellow practical-light remnant if needed. Clear strong subject, only 2–4 gameplay-relevant details, left/upper-left low-detail negative space for UI. No people required, no decorative apocalypse spectacle, no neon, no cinematic blockbuster grading, no text, no readable signage, no logo, no watermark.

## A05 — 半开的卷帘门 / Half-open Shutter — Key Event Master

### Event idea

The search team reaches the convenience store. Supplies are close, but the half-open shutter forces a vulnerable entry and something in the rear storage area is making intermittent noise.

### Visual rule

Do **not** depict a named core character in this anchor. The event can be triggered by different player-selected expedition members. Use anonymous cropped civilian bodies / hands so the illustration remains systemically valid.

### Chosen instant

One survivor has one hand under the half-open shutter, holding it slightly higher. Another crouches just outside. A weak flashlight beam reaches only partway across the floor toward the dark storage doorway. The audience understands: food is visible, but entry commits the team.

### Composition

- landscape 16:9 preferred
- viewpoint near ground height but not extreme wide-angle
- shutter forms a strong horizontal pressure line
- one or two anonymous civilian partial figures, faces obscured or outside identity-reading range
- visible food packages just beyond reach
- dark storage doorway in secondary focal position
- preserve one side / upper region with low-detail shadow for UI

### Rendering prompt

Tense single instant during a civilian scavenging run at a small ruined neighborhood convenience store: a metal rolling shutter is jammed half-open, one ordinary survivor's dusty hand lifts it a little higher while another civilian crouches outside, both dressed in plain worn civilian layers with no military gear and no identifiable hero design. A weak flashlight beam enters beneath the shutter and reveals a few sealed food packets and tins just beyond reach, then fades before the dark rear storage doorway where an unseen intermittent noise might be coming from. The emotional conflict is immediate: supplies are visible, but crawling inside is vulnerable. Documentary wartime field-journal event illustration, charcoal sketch, graphite, rough ink, cross-hatching, rubbed shadows, weathered paper, high-but-controlled contrast, low saturation charcoal black, concrete gray, dirty beige, cold blue-gray, tiny dim tungsten yellow flashlight accent, optional very small dark rust-red smear. Strong body language, no gore, no monster close-up, no cinematic action poster, no heroic pose, no tactical gear, no anime, no glossy CGI. Preserve a low-detail dark safe zone for UI title/body/choices. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## A06 — 宿营屋 Lv1 / Improvised Shelter — Main Building Master

### Gameplay identity

The shelter already exists at DAY 1. It is not comfortable and not a base fortress. It is the first proof that ordinary people have managed to make a damaged place livable.

### Mandatory visual structure

Main subject:
- former street-front room / ground-floor residential-commercial space converted into a communal sleeping and cooking shelter

Narrative details:
- patched window using wood board + translucent plastic sheet
- two or three thin mattresses / folded blankets against a wall
- improvised low cooking corner with blackened pot / kettle
- salvaged shelf holding neatly rationed small supplies
- one weak bulb or stove glow as the only warm focal accent

Construction language:
- mismatched boards
- wire
- tape
- scavenged hinges
- repaired chair/table
- repeated repairs visible

No defensive fortification dominance. This is a human living space first.

### Composition

- landscape 3:2
- eye-level corner view, slightly wide but natural
- foreground shows hand-built repair language
- midground shows sleeping + cooking coexistence
- small warm pool of light inside a mostly cold gray room
- reserve upper wall / one side as calmer UI-safe area

### Rendering prompt

Improvised civilian shelter inside a damaged ground-floor street-front room after several weeks of urban disaster, ordinary people have converted a former residential-commercial space into somewhere barely livable. Dominant structure: patched broken window repaired with mismatched salvaged wood boards and translucent plastic sheet held by wire, tape and crude fasteners. Two or three thin mattresses and folded mismatched blankets against cracked plaster wall, small low cooking corner with blackened pot and kettle, salvaged shelf with a few carefully rationed supplies, repaired chair and scratched table, visible repeated fixes and reused hinges. One weak old tungsten bulb or tiny stove glow forms the only warm focal area inside an otherwise cold concrete-gray room. No large military barricades, no command center, no fortress aesthetic. Wartime civilian survival chronicle, charcoal and graphite, rough ink, hand-drawn cross-hatching, rubbed shadows, weathered paper fibers, chipped plaster, old wood, rusted metal, patched cloth, dust, emotional realism, low saturation charcoal black, concrete gray, dirty beige, weathered brown, cold blue-gray, small dim yellow hope accent. Eye-level natural documentary perspective, calm negative space on an upper wall/side for UI. No people required or only indistinct background human presence, no text, no logos, no watermark.

# Anchor acceptance checklist

The six images pass only if they feel like pages from **one single wartime survival sketchbook**.

Reject the set if:

- A01 and A02 feel rendered by different art pipelines
- locations look more polished than characters
- event art becomes a movie poster
- shelter looks like a military base
- any image becomes colorful at thumbnail size
- any person looks like a fashion model, soldier or game hero
- paper/graphite/charcoal language disappears under photorealistic polish

After approval, these six anchors become image references for Phase 2 production.
